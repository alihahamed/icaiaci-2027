Welcome

Welcome to ICAIACI–2027

The International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Computational Intelligence (ICAIACI–2027) aims to provide a premier international platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, innovators, and students to exchange cutting-edge research findings, emerging trends, and practical applications in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Advanced Computational Intelligence.

The conference seeks to foster interdisciplinary collaboration, promote innovation-driven research, and facilitate academia–industry partnerships to address contemporary scientific, technological, and societal challenges.

01 / About

About the Conference

Artificial Intelligence and Computational Intelligence are transforming diverse sectors, including healthcare, agriculture, manufacturing, finance, education, cybersecurity, transportation, and smart cities.

Recent advances in Generative AI, Machine Learning, Explainable AI, Intelligent Automation, Edge Intelligence, Foundation Models, and Autonomous Systems have accelerated digital transformation across industries worldwide.

ICAIACI–2027 will bring together eminent international experts and researchers to deliberate on these advances and enable participants to gain insights into global research directions, emerging technologies, ethical AI practices, and sustainable AI solutions.

In the national context, the conference aligns with India's vision of becoming a global leader in AI-driven innovation through initiatives such as the IndiaAI Mission, Digital India, Atmanirbhar Bharat, and National Education Policy (NEP) 2020.

The conference provides a platform for researchers and students to present innovative solutions addressing national priorities in areas including smart healthcare, precision agriculture, intelligent transportation, disaster management, cybersecurity, language technologies, and inclusive digital governance.

Objectives

ICAIACI–2027 aims to:

  1. Provide an international platform for presenting high-quality research in Artificial Intelligence and Computational Intelligence.
  2. Promote interaction among researchers, academicians, industry professionals, innovators, and students.
  3. Facilitate interdisciplinary and collaborative research.
  4. Encourage innovation-driven solutions to real-world problems.
  5. Strengthen academia–industry interaction.
  6. Promote responsible, ethical, and sustainable Artificial Intelligence.
  7. Provide young researchers and students exposure to emerging global research trends.
  8. Create opportunities for future collaborative research, publications, internships, and academic partnerships.

08 / The Host

About Yenepoya Institute of Technology

Yenepoya Institute of Technology (YIT) is located at Thodar, Moodbidri, Mangaluru, Karnataka.

The institution promotes technical education, research, innovation, entrepreneurship, student development, and industry–academia interaction. Through technical events, hackathons, coding competitions, research activities, and innovation initiatives, the institution provides opportunities for students and faculty members to engage with emerging technologies and real-world challenges.

Previous Technical Activities

Matrix Fusion – AI & Coding HackathonAn AI and coding hackathon organized to bring students together to solve real-world problems using Artificial Intelligence and programming skills. Participants worked in teams to develop innovative solutions while enhancing coding, problem-solving, and teamwork abilities.

Bytecode 3.0 – Interdepartmental Coding CompetitionA two-day coding competition involving students from different departments. The event promoted algorithmic thinking, competitive coding, collaboration, and technical excellence.

Yentechmania – Annual Technical FestYIT's technical festival featuring hackathons, coding contests, technical talks, and IoT/Embedded ideation activities. The event encourages creativity, innovation, prototype development, and practical problem-solving.
